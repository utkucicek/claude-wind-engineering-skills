# Reference — Chapter 9: Report content and Chapter 10: Project peer review

Source: ASCE "Prestandard for Use of Computational Wind Engineering in Building Design", Chapters 9 and 10.
"PDF page" = PDF sequence number (printed page = PDF − 18).
Type: MANDATORY = "shall", RECOMMENDED = "should / recommended", COMMENTARY = "Commentary" or explanatory text.
These two chapters have no PO/AC-numbered clauses; the requirements are given by subsection number. There are almost no numerical thresholds.

## Chapter 10 — Project peer review

| Clause | Type | Summary | Numerical criterion | PDF page |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| §1.7 | MANDATORY | Use of CWE to determine MWFRS, C&C and appurtenance loads requires peer review; requirements in Chapter 10. | none | 24 |
| §10.1 (a) | MANDATORY | When the provisions of this Prestandard are used, an independent peer review is performed. | none | 95 |
| §10.1 (b) | MANDATORY | The review is performed by an expert firm or panel of individuals with experience and knowledge in the following: (1) computational flow simulation including atmospheric boundary layer flow; (2) wind engineering and flow around bluff bodies; (3) if relevant, dynamic response of flexible structures in wind; (4) wind hazard definition (design speeds and directions depending on the exceedance probability); (5) exposure determination; (6) wind load determination by wind tunnel modeling; (7) the Prestandard requirements on the development and execution of the simulation domain. | 7 competence areas | 95 |
| §10.1 commentary | COMMENTARY | The peer review process is an indispensable component of quality control in design load estimation with CWE. | none | 95 |
| §10.2 (a) | MANDATORY | The design team selects the peer reviewer/panel and notifies the AHJ of its selection for general concurrence. | none | 95 |
| §10.2 (b) | MANDATORY | The peer reviewer has no conflict of interest related to the project and is not part of the project design team; is fully independent of the design team and of the sponsor of the simulation. | none | 95 |
| §10.2 (c) | MANDATORY | If the peer review body consists of more than one person, one of them chairs. The chair is responsible for mediating disagreements between the peer reviewers, for keeping the record of the review and for communicating the official opinions of the team. | none | 95 |
| §10.2 commentary | COMMENTARY / RECOMMENDED | The composition of the peer reviewer or panel should generally be determined by the owner and the design team (EOR + simulation team), together with the building authority if it is being done on behalf of the AHJ. There is no specific recommendation for the panel composition; expertise in the §10.1 areas must be present. Some jurisdictions may require the chair to be licensed in that jurisdiction; this is not a general requirement of the Prestandard. | none | 95–96 |
| §10.3 (a) | MANDATORY | The scope of the review is discussed among the owner, the design team, the peer review team and, to the extent appropriate, the AHJ. The peer reviewer(s) prepare(s) a written scope of work in the contract. | none | 96 |
| §10.3-1 | MANDATORY | Review of the basis of design document: done as the first step, before the full simulation; so that the comments are addressed before the final simulation. The document summarizes, at a minimum, how the elements in the Prestandard will be addressed. | none | 96 |
| §10.3-2 | MANDATORY | Review of the wind hazard determination and of the simulation technique to be used to find the loads and response. | none | 96 |
| §10.3-3 | MANDATORY | Review of the simulation setup, the inputs and the domain generation; of the simulation performance; of the results; determination of whether the computed loads and responses meet the approved acceptance criteria. | none | 96 |
| §10.3-4 | MANDATORY | Review of the simulation results and their evaluation with respect to the stated performance objectives and acceptance criteria. | none | 96 |
| §10.3-5 | MANDATORY | Review of the evaluation of the estimated design wind loads to be used in the structural design and of the other outputs relevant to the scope. | none | 96 |
| §10.3-6 | MANDATORY | Other matters considered important for meeting the established performance objectives. | none | 96 |
| §10.3-7 | MANDATORY | Peer reviewer comments: the peer reviewer(s) convey(s) the technical comments to the design team, also indicating possible resolutions and matters requiring further discussion. | none | 96 |
| §10.3 (end) | COMMENTARY (permission) | At the discretion of the AHJ and the other members of the development team, the scope may be extended to include other matters such as the wind design of critical nonstructural elements. | none | 96 |
| §10.4 | MANDATORY | A meeting is held among the EOR, the team performing the simulation, (if appropriate) the AHJ and the peer reviewers: the scope of work, the methods and lines of communication, the frequency and timing of the review stages, and the expected level of design development at each stage are established. | none | 96–97 |
| §10.4 commentary | RECOMMENDED | The peer review process should be started at the earliest possible stage of the design; early concurrence avoids rework. The scope, the schedule and special communication/delivery requirements should be discussed at the kickoff meeting. The review and response durations should be put into the project design schedule. | none (no duration given) | 97 |
| §10.5 | MANDATORY | The peer reviewer is given documents organized in a way that facilitates the review. One of these is the Computational Wind Simulation Report, whose content is based on the Chapter 9 requirements; it is submitted in stages for early review of the basis of design and of the simulation parameters and assumptions. The peer reviewers give timely written comments, with action requests where necessary, to the EOR, the simulation team and, if necessary, the AHJ. The design team is responsible for resolving all comments to the satisfaction of the peer reviewers. | none | 97 |
| §10.5 commentary | RECOMMENDED | It is good practice for the simulation team to hold a meeting with the peer reviewers after each submission and introduce the documents. The peer reviewers are given reasonable time to prepare a comment log; the design team should respond in writing, and several rounds may be needed on important matters. It is ideal to keep a schedule (usually a spreadsheet) in which the comments and resolutions are recorded with their dates; the conclusions reached in meetings and telephone calls should also be written there. | none | 97 |
| §10.6 | MANDATORY | At the end of the review and at other times requested by the owner or the AHJ, the peer review team submits a written report: the scope of the review, the comment log and the professional opinion of the peer reviewers on the general compliance of the simulation with the performance objective and acceptance criterion requirements of the Prestandard. | 3 content items | 97 |
| §10.7 | MANDATORY (responsibility provision) | The responsibility for determining the wind load and the wind response of the structure belongs solely to the wind engineer who performs the computational wind simulation. | none | 98 |
| §10.7 commentary | COMMENTARY / RECOMMENDED | Peer review does not relieve the simulation team of its professional responsibilities and does not replace the team's normal quality assurance measures. The reports and documents produced in the peer review process are not construction documents; they should not be attached to the project drawings and should not be reproduced in a way that makes them appear to be part of the contract documents. The peer review documents should be kept in the project files of the building authority. | none | 98 |
| §10.8 | MANDATORY | If a disagreement between the responsible designers and the peer reviewers cannot be resolved in the normal review process, other experts are retained to review the material and recommend a way forward. These additional experts meet the same requirements as the original peer review team. | none | 98 |
| §10.8 commentary | COMMENTARY | Disagreements may arise from the wind climate assumptions, the features of the simulation, the nature of the results or other aspects of the application of the Davenport wind engineering chain; the chain is a useful tool for isolating particular aspects of the simulation and requesting additional information. | none | 98 |
| §10.9 | MANDATORY | If a substantial change occurs in the building design after the peer review is completed, the EOR informs the AHJ, describing the changes in the architectural or structural design. At the discretion of the AHJ these changes may be subject to additional review by the peer review team and to AHJ approval. | none | 98 |
| §10.9 commentary | COMMENTARY | The EOR is responsible for notifying the AHJ of changes that are reasonably suspected of being able to affect the wind loads or the response. Examples of substantial changes: architectural shape or massing; changes in the size and/or layout of the lateral load resisting system that alter the stiffness or the mass distribution; changes in the inherent or supplementary damping of the structure. | none | 98 |

## Unreadable / missing items

- There is no unreadable equation or corrupted table in Chapters 9 and 10.
- §9.3.3.4 refers to "Chapter 8" for the acceptance criteria; Chapter 8 contains only Table 8-1 (the spectrum criteria are the Chapter 5/6 rows in the table).
- §9.2.2 says "near flow region" and "far-field region"; the region names in Table 8-1 and the §1.8 definitions are in the form "near-wall", "local flow", "approach flow", "background/far flow". Reproduced as is.
- Appendix: not found in the text; after Chapter 10 come the References (PDF p. 99) and the Index.
- Numerical requirement for the number of peer reviewers, a licensing requirement, review durations: not found in the text.
