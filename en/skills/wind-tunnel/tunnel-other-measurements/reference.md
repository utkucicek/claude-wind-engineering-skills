# Reference — other measurements in the wind tunnel (ASCE/SEI 49-21)

Source: ASCE/SEI 49-21, §1.1, §1.4, §3.4.2, §3.5, §4, §5, §7, §8.1 and the related commentary sections (C2.3.2, C3.4.2, C3.5, C4, C5, C7). Page = PDF page order.

Type column: **MANDATORY** = "shall" in the standard text; **MANDATORY (permitted)** = "shall be permitted"; **COMMENTARY** = commentary (not binding); **COMMENTARY (recommendation)** = "should / recommended" in the commentary.

## Not found in the text

- A criterion, measurement method or threshold for pedestrian-level wind comfort/safety: none (out of scope, §1.1).
- A numerical minimum system natural frequency for the HFFB: none (only the general "do not contaminate the data" requirement and the 30% margin in §8.1).
- A frequency limit for the rigid / flexible / aeroelastic classes: none (the commentary refers to the frequency limit in ASCE 7 but gives no value).
- The size of the margin to be sought above the ULS speed for the instability onset speed: none.
- A mandatory number for the section model length/width ratio: none (only about 4–5 in the commentary).
